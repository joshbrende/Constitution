## Longer notice (provincial bulletin)

### Subject: Phase 1 pilot — Constitution & Academy platform

Comrades,

National ICT has activated the **ZANU PF Constitution & Academy** platform for **Harare** and **Bulawayo** metropolitan provinces.

**What members should do**

1. **Get the app** — Android / iOS (store links on the public website when published) or use the web portal.
2. **Register** — Province can be added during profile completion if not set at signup.
3. **Complete profile** — Select your province and enter your national ID (required for certification).
4. **Academy** — Enrol in the membership course, study offline where possible, and sit the assessment.
5. **Certificate** — After passing, pay the course fee at your provincial office. Provincial admin confirms payment; **Presidium** approves before physical certificate collection.

**Provincial offices**

- Ensure a designated **payment collection point** and receipt process.
- Provincial admin accounts are provisioned by national ICT — contact your **provincial chair** or ICT focal person.

**Pilot duration:** 90 days. Feedback will shape national rollout.
